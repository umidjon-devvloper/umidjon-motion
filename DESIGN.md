# Umidjon Motion — full-page design

Direction: one continuous cinematic black space, acid lime focal points, violet edges, reflective glass panels. Match the existing hero and Creative Universe references through spacing, materials and type hierarchy.

## Page sequence

1. Hero and portfolio: retain the accepted layout and background motion.
2. Cinematic showcase: a wide campaign image with a dark caption gradient, luminous frame and concept label.
3. Services: centered heading, six deep glass cards, enlarged dimensional service symbols, violet/lime edge lighting, clear service links.
4. About: split composition with a studio emblem below the heading and numbered capability panels.
5. Process: four connected production stages, large stage numbers, dark panels and luminous markers.
6. Pricing: three packages, a lime illuminated center package, violet side packages, consistent full-width quote buttons. Preserve custom pricing and actual package details.
7. Final invitation: centered type inside a large tilted neon orbit, no foreground object covering copy.
8. Contact: left-aligned introduction and channels, a framed dark form on the right, clear field states.
9. Footer: oversized outline wordmark, compact section navigation, social links and back-to-top link.

## Rules

Background #07080c; lime #c8ff42; violet #a36bff. White headings and readable cool-gray body copy. Headings scale from 32 to 68px; panels use 24–30px corners. Keep ambient halos outside reading areas. Use restrained hover lift and slow orbit motion; disable ambient animation for reduced-motion preferences. Stack cards and split layouts on narrow screens. Preserve contact behavior, concept disclosures and existing navigation.
